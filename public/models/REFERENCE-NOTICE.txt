Experimental reference matching data for Signsprout
Reference teaching media: ASL University, Dr. William G. Vicars, Lifeprint.com. Copyright remains with the author. Original URLs are recorded in app/media.json and public/demos/ATTRIBUTION.txt.
Alphabet: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/fingerspelling/abc.htm
Landmark extraction: MediaPipe Hands/Holistic, Google LLC, Apache-2.0.
Static templates are landmark representations extracted from the displayed reference photographs at multiple orientations. H is an explicitly geometric template derived from the adjacent extended fingers of U directed sideways; it is not an extracted H measurement.
Word templates contain body-relative hand locations and handshape sequences extracted from demonstration GIFs/videos and frame sequences. PLEASE, MY, YOU and 10 use authored geometric rules instead. J and Z require shape and trajectory checks.
These reference examples do not establish accuracy on independent signers. No population accuracy is claimed. Facial grammar and fluency are not graded.
